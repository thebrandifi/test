What is product advertisement videos?

A product ad has seconds to win attention. We open with a strong first-second hook, show the product clearly and finish with a call to action that tells the viewer what to do next.

Our product ads include a digital indoor antenna for HLB Plux and a fragrance and handbag ad for Chadoff Collections. Both were made as vertical videos for social media.

What you get

  • A hook, message and call to action built around your product
  • Product shots, animation and effects produced with AI tools, using your photos where you have them
  • Captions and sound designed for viewers watching on phones
  • Vertical 9:16 format, with other sizes on request
  • Optional ad running, so we also place the video in front of customers

Who it is for

Online stores, retailers, fashion and beauty brands and anyone selling a product who needs advertising that looks professional without a studio shoot.

How it works

  1. Brief

    Tell us your goal, audience and deadline. We will confirm the plan and quote before we start.

  2. Plan

    You approve the idea, look and message before we build.

  3. Create

    We produce the video and deliver it in the formats you need.

  4. Review and refine

    We review the result with you and adjust until it is right.

Questions about product advertisement videos

Can you make an ad from my product photos?

Yes. Our HLB Plux ad was built with real product photos.

Can you also run the ads?

Yes. The Video + ads option covers making the video and running the ads for you. Ad budget is paid separately.

What format are product ads?

Mostly vertical 9:16 for Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. We can supply other sizes.
