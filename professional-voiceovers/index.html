What is professional voiceovers?

Voice carries a message. A well-paced voiceover makes information easier to follow, builds trust and gives a video its personality. Our videos are made to be heard as well as seen, which is why the previews on this site play with sound.

We can add a voiceover to a video we make, or to a video you already have.

What you get

  • A voiceover for your script, or help improving the script
  • A voice and tone chosen to suit your brand and audience
  • Timing matched to the visuals
  • Clean audio ready for social media and ads
  • Review and refinement before delivery

Who it is for

Businesses with silent videos or slide presentations, and brands that want a consistent voice across promos, explainers and ads.

How it works

  1. Brief

    Tell us your goal, audience and deadline. We will confirm the plan and quote before we start.

  2. Plan

    You approve the idea, look and message before we build.

  3. Create

    We produce the video and deliver it in the formats you need.

  4. Review and refine

    We review the result with you and adjust until it is right.

Questions about professional voiceovers

Can you voice a video I already made?

Yes. Send the video and the script and we add the voiceover.

Can you help write the script?

Yes. We can tidy your script or write one with you.

Do voiceovers come with captions?

We can add captions so the video works with the sound off.
