How to write a video brief

A video brief is a short description of what you want your video to achieve. The clearer it is, the closer the first version will be to what you imagined.

Published 2026-11-24 by BrandiFi · 4 min read

1. The goal

Say what the video should do. Introduce your business, sell a product, explain a service or get people to message you? One main goal produces a stronger video than three.

2. The audience

Describe who will watch. Their age, interests and where they spend time online all shape the style, language and length.

3. The main message

If viewers remember one thing, what should it be? Write it in a single sentence.

4. The call to action

Decide what viewers should do next, such as messaging you on WhatsApp, visiting your shop or booking an appointment. The video should end by saying it.

5. Length and format

Most social media videos work well when they are short and vertical (9:16). Tell us where the video will be shown: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, your website or WhatsApp.

6. Your brand assets

Share your logo, brand colours and fonts, and any product photos or videos you want to use. We build around what you already have.

7. Examples you like

Send links to videos you admire and tell us what you like about them. They are a quick way to share a style.

8. Deadline and budget

Tell us when you need the video and what you want to spend. We will confirm a quote before we begin.

Do not have an idea yet?

You do not need a finished brief. If you only know your business and your goal, tell us that. We develop the concept and script with you.

A short sample brief

Goal: introduce our new product range. Audience: women 25 to 40 in Accra. Message: natural products for everyday wellness. Call to action: message us on WhatsApp to order. Format: vertical, 30 to 40 seconds, for Instagram and TikTok. Assets: logo, colours and product photos attached. Deadline: two weeks.
