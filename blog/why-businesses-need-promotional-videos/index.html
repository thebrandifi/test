Why every business needs a promotional video

A promotional video is the quickest way to show people what your business does and why they should choose you. Here is why it earns its place.

Published 2026-11-24 by BrandiFi · 3 min read

It explains your business without reading

Most people will watch a short video before they read a page of text. A clear promo introduces your service, your style and your offer in under a minute.

It builds trust

Seeing your brand, hearing your voice and watching your service explained makes a business feel real. For clinics, shops and service providers, that confidence is often the first step to an enquiry.

You can use it everywhere

One video can be posted on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, shared on WhatsApp, placed on your website and used in adverts. A vertical 9:16 version suits phones best.

It makes advertising work harder

Video ads can be shown to people who are likely to be interested in what you offer. A strong first second and a clear call to action help viewers decide whether to stop scrolling.

What makes a promotional video work

  • A strong hook. Give viewers a reason to keep watching in the first second.
  • One message. Say one thing clearly instead of everything at once.
  • A call to action. End by saying what to do next.
  • Sound and captions. Many people watch with the sound off, so captions help; a good voiceover helps the rest.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to explain every service in one video.
  • No call to action at the end.
  • Videos made for a desktop screen instead of phones.
