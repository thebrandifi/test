What is promotional videos?

A promotional video introduces your business, service or offer in under a minute. It works on social media, on your website and in WhatsApp conversations, because people can understand your value without reading.

We build promo videos around one clear message and one call to action, so viewers know what you offer and how to reach you.

What you get

  • A concept, script and storyboard matched to your offer
  • Visuals, animation and voiceover produced with AI tools
  • Your logo, colours and contact details on screen
  • A call to action, such as messaging you on WhatsApp
  • Review and refinement before delivery

Who it is for

Businesses launching or promoting a service, shop, clinic, event or brand, and entrepreneurs who need a clear introduction they can share anywhere.

How it works

  1. Brief

    Tell us your goal, audience and deadline. We will confirm the plan and quote before we start.

  2. Plan

    You approve the idea, look and message before we build.

  3. Create

    We produce the video and deliver it in the formats you need.

  4. Review and refine

    We review the result with you and adjust until it is right.

Questions about promotional videos

How long is a promotional video?

It depends on the goal. Most of our promos are between 30 and 50 seconds, which suits social media.

Can you promote a service rather than a product?

Yes. We have made promos for health centres, a recruitment agency and retail brands.

What do you need from me?

Your goal, your audience, your logo and any photos or videos you already have. We guide you through the rest.
